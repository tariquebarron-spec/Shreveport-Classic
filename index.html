Shreveport Classic * Official name pending

Shreveport Classic presented by the Shreveport Classic Foundation will take place at Independence Stadium on October 23, 2027 at 12 PM.

Event Details

Inaugural Shreveport Classic presented by the Shreveport Classic Foundation between four storied football programs, the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff and Grambling State University followed by Southern University and Jackson State University, on October 23, 2027, at Independence Stadium.

Held the week before the State Fair, the Classic anchors four days of vendor fairs, forums, parades, parties and band showcases across Shreveport–Bossier.

The official tailgate opens at seven and stays open between games. Grills, alumni tents, a kids zone and a vendor village stretch across the lots, giving fans a place to land for the full day of football and a reason to stay between kickoffs.

The Classic parade steps off at eight with high school and college bands, dance lines, Greek organizations, floats and city officials. The route runs the Boardwalk and ends at the stadium gates just as the tailgate lots hit full stride.
